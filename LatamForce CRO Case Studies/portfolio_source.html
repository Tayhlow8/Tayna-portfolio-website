L'OR Origins · National QR Campaign

Origin-mapped coffee,
from shelf to screen

Strategy & Objective

Launch a premium, origin-mapped coffee line in Brazil ("Sul de Minas" and "Mogiana"). The goal: spark nationwide digital awareness that ultimately converts users into buyers at physical brick-and-mortar retail.

The Omnichannel Loop

Product packaging carried a prominent QR code across supermarket shelves nationwide — the primary offline-to-online bridge directly to this digital destination.

UX & CRO Interventions

Sensorial storytelling. An immersive dark-mode environment built to mimic a luxury digital tasting — high-contrast type and rich profiles emphasizing aroma, body and acidity.

Strategic action paths. "Experimente" CTAs routed users to retail locators and partner delivery apps rather than a DTC checkout — matching the awareness-first goal.

3
Brasil em Gotas · Premium Fashion E-Commerce

Killing the hidden-step
PDP that stalled checkout

The Problem

A funnel analysis revealed a critical leak: a bounce rate exceeding 70% directly on the Product Detail Page (PDP), stalling users right before they could initiate checkout.

UX & CRO Interventions

The sequential-journey trap. The interface forced users to click a color swatch before size options appeared. This hidden state caused cognitive friction and erratic, drop-off behavior.

Qualitative verification. Cross-referenced with Hotjar clickmaps & heatmaps. Even with explicit "1. Selecione a cor primeiro" instructions, users missed the dependency entirely.

A/B testing structure. A 2-week VWO experiment, 50/50 traffic. Control kept the hidden sequence; the Variant exposed all color + size options simultaneously on load.

L'Oréal Group · DermaClub · Checkout Optimization

Rebuilding a broken
checkout on VTEX

Key Metrics · 2-week A/B, 50/50
MetricGrowth impact
Revenue uplift+7.10%
Conversion rate (CR)+5.71%
Checkout click-through (CTR)+32.00%
UX & CRO Interventions

The funnel friction. 70% of users reached the cart, but momentum broke at the gateway — an 80% bounce inside checkout, driven by VTEX rendering bugs and endless scroll.

Data-backed redesign. Heuristic audits + Hotjar attention maps exposed a fragmented hierarchy — users hunting for totals, coupon fields and loyalty points at once.

Reducing cognitive load. A clean high-contrast light surface, progressive disclosure of loyalty milestones, and a unified styling matrix to group items within VTEX's container limits.
